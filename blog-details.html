CITY RIDING / 8 MIN READ

Seven kilometres.
Three ways.

A practical city ride is not just the shortest line. It is the route that balances flow, range, parking and a simple return.

RIDEY EDITORIAL15 SEP 2026
01
START WITH THE TRIP

A short journey can still deserve a plan.

The useful route balances time, road character, parking and the scooter’s remaining range. A few seconds of planning before pickup can remove most of the friction later.

“The fastest line on a map is not always the simplest ride.”
02 / ROUTE CHOICE

Choose for flow,
not just distance.

Compare the direct road with a calmer parallel route. A slightly longer line can reduce stops, difficult turns and last-minute lane changes.

  • DirectFastest on paper, often busier.
  • CalmMore predictable junctions and pace.
  • FlexibleEasier places to pause or reroute.

Think of range as
a decision margin.

Displayed range is a planning signal, not a target to use completely. Leave room for traffic, a detour or a different return point.
