GOOD TO KNOW

A little clarity before you go.

Scooter or bike: which should I choose?

Choose an automatic scooter for short city journeys. For longer routes, consider a bike suited to your licence, riding experience and route. Confirm outstation permission before booking.

Should I choose a weekly or monthly plan?

Weekly suits a short stay or a busy work week. Monthly suits regular commuting. Compare the total rental, included kilometres and deposit on the pricing page.

What do I need at pickup?

Bring a valid driving licence and the identification requested in your booking. Confirm helmet availability and inspect your ride before leaving.

Can I take my rental outside the city?

Check permitted areas, kilometre limits and return requirements with the rental team before planning an outstation trip.
