04 / PLAN WITH CONFIDENCE

Before you
choose a plan.

A few answers to help you compare your options.

Which plan suits a short stay?

Daily suits one-off trips; weekly suits several days of local travel. Compare your total rental and kilometre allowance, rather than only the headline rate.

Are these rates for every vehicle?

These are example starting city-scooter rates. Bikes and other models can have different prices. Confirm the exact quotation with the rental team.

Is the deposit included in the estimate?

No. The estimate shows only the example base rental charge. The deposit and any other applicable costs are confirmed separately.

Can I change my rental period?

Check extension availability, charges and cancellation terms with the rental team before changing the agreed period.
