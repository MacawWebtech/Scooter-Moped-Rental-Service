05 / IF YOU’RE UNSURE, ASK

Clear answers
before the keys.

Is the deposit the same for every ride?

It can depend on the vehicle and rental terms. Confirm the exact amount before making a booking.

Does checking this list confirm my rental?

No. These checkboxes are personal reminders in this page only. They do not confirm a booking, vehicle inspection or payment.

What should I do if I notice an issue at pickup?

Raise it with the rental team before accepting the vehicle and ask for the condition to be recorded.

Who should I contact about a return or refund?

Use the contact provided with your rental agreement and have your booking details and payment receipt ready.
