04 / ONE LAST CHECK

Is it the right
service for you?

Would a longer rental suit me better?

If you will ride on several days, compare a weekly rental with separate daily bookings. Check the total cost, distance allowance and return terms.

Can I extend my booking?

Check with the rental team before the agreed return time. Extensions depend on availability and the applicable charges.

What should I bring to pickup?

Bring your valid driving licence and the identification requested for your booking. Confirm any additional requirements with the team.
